This installs OscillaFX, a system-wide audio enhancer:

  • OscillaFX.app in /Applications
  • The OscillaFX Audio virtual device in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL

Upgrading from Oscilla replaces the old app and driver; your profiles and settings are kept.

Quit eqMac, FxSound or other audio enhancers first. System audio drops for about two seconds while Core Audio restarts.
